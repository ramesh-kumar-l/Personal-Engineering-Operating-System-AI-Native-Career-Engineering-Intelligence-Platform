# 04 — User Personas

Status: ACTIVE (partially validated)
Last updated: 2026-09-19 (Phase 0)

## Persona 1 — The creator (FACT, primary)

Ramesh Kumar L, the repo owner. A senior engineer who builds with AI coding agents, maintains roughly 70 project folders under `D:\ClaudeProjects\` (per `PROJECTS.md`, 2026-09-18), prepares for Staff-level interviews, and wants engineering work converted into durable evidence and public artifacts.

Daily loop the product must serve (spec §87): before work → prioritize; during work → provide context; after work → capture evidence; weekly → learn; interview prep → retrieve real experience; public work → generate artifacts.

Observed pain (INFERENCE from `PROJECTS.md`): context and attention are fragmented across many overlapping, mostly paused projects. The product's first job is to reduce that fragmentation for this user, not add to it.

## Persona 2 — Senior/Staff/Principal engineer using AI coding tools (ASSUMPTION)

Experiences engineering-context overload; needs trustworthy context for agents, memory of decisions, and evidence of impact. Status: unvalidated. No interviews or design partners yet. Do not build persona-2-specific features until at least one such engineer has used the product.

## Secondary personas (spec §5, LATER)

Engineers preparing for advancement, AI engineers, platform/infrastructure engineers, engineering managers, AI-heavy teams. All ASSUMPTION.

## CONSTRAINT

Do not overfit architecture to one technology stack or discipline (spec §5). Backend, frontend, mobile, AI/ML, infra, security, data, embedded, platform engineers must all be supportable.
